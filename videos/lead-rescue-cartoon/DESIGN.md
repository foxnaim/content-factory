# Design truth

Concept angle: a warm night-shift toy workshop where a tiny robot repairs the path of a living star-shaped lead.

- Focal elements: the teal robot and golden star.
- Edge anchors: pipes, shelves and softly glowing machinery keep the frame grounded without competing with the characters.
- Supporting detail: route dots, alert bell, gears, confetti and a home-shaped exit.
- Background: deep navy with warm amber pools of light.
- Palette: navy `#071521`, teal `#4fd8c7`, gold `#ffd166`, coral `#ff7b70`, cream `#fff7df`.
- Typography: heavy Nunito for rounded child-friendly display text and regular Nunito for captions.
- Motion: character-led action, short anticipation, clean pushes, bounded bobs and deterministic particle bursts.
- Safety: captions remain inside the center safe area; no claim, metric or external asset is invented.
