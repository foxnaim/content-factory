# Lead Rescue

A short children's-style animated proof that turns a dry CRM failure into a simple story. A friendly teal robot helps a lost golden lead find a clear path through assignment, alerts, logging and follow-up.

The viewer should understand that a lead without an owner and next action can disappear, while a connected process gives every lead a clear way home.

The piece uses an original vector world, a local Kokoro neural narrator, English captions and small original sound cues. It is a local review draft and has no publishing action.
